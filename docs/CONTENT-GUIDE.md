# Editorial & Citation Verification Guide — Red Sea Monitor

This document outlines the strict editorial policies, scientific verification standards, and source citation rules for Red Sea Monitor.

## 1. Core Editorial Philosophy: "A Field Journal, Not a Template"
Red Sea Monitor operates with the rigorous fact-checking standards of an academic scientific journal coupled with investigative journalism ethics.
- **No Unverified Rumors:** Sighting and conservation news must cite primary research papers, official EEAA/NIOF decrees, or verified field researcher logs.
- **No Sponsored Tourism Hype:** Avoid promotional hotel or dive center PR copy. Focus on ecological integrity, safety, scientific observation, and responsible visitor behavior.

## 2. Mandatory Sourcing Rules for News & Investigations
- **Zero-Source Policy:** A `NEWS` article **CANNOT** be published without at least one verified external source URL. The editorial workspace enforces this rule before allowing the status change to `PUBLISHED`.
- **Source Integrity:** Editors must record:
  1. Primary Publisher (e.g. *Nature Scientific Reports*, *NIOF*, *EEAA*)
  2. Direct URL or DOI
  3. Original Publication Date
  4. Date Accessed by RSM Editorial Team

## 3. Taxonomic & IUCN Integrity
- **Scientific Binomial Nomenclature:** Always use italics for genus and species (*Chelonia mydas*, *Dugong dugon*).
- **IUCN Red List Status:** Must be cross-referenced with the latest IUCN Red List version. Status categories:
  - `CR`: Critically Endangered
  - `EN`: Endangered
  - `VU`: Vulnerable
  - `NT`: Near Threatened
  - `LC`: Least Concern
  - `DD`: Data Deficient
  - `NE`: Not Evaluated

## 4. Corrections Policy
If factual inaccuracies, misidentifications, or outdated telemetry data occur:
1. Append an entry to `ArticleCorrection` via the Admin Article editor.
2. The correction log appears transparently at the foot of the published article with timestamp and editor attribution.

## 5. Verification Markers in Seed Content
- Anything in seed/demo data requiring field biologist confirmation is flagged with `TODO: verify` in our verification tracker.
