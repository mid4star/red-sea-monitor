# Editorial & Verification Standards — Red Sea Monitor

**Version:** 2.0  
**Last Updated:** October 2026 (Phase B Regional Basin Expansion)  
**Governing Principle:** Verifiable Field Science, Pan-Basin Ecological Unity, and Absolute Fact Transparency

---

## 1. The Zero-Source News Ban
An article categorized as `NEWS` **cannot be published** on Red Sea Monitor without at least one verifiable external source URL. This rule is strictly enforced at the schema and application layer.
* Unsubstantiated claims, commercial press releases, and viral social media rumors are excluded.
* Acceptable primary sources include: peer-reviewed journals (with DOIs), official government park decrees (EEAA, NCW, ASEZA, WCGA), and recognized intergovernmental organizations (IUCN, PERSGA, UNESCO).

---

## 2. Scientific Verification & Reviewer Attribution
Gazetteer entries, species taxonomy profiles, and wildlife sightings undergo peer review by registered oceanographers, rangers, and ecologists. Every published dispatch records:
* **Verified By:** The name, credential, role, and institution of the reviewing editor.
* **Last Verified Date:** Timestamp of factual re-audit.
* **Persistent Identifiers:** DOIs for scientific papers; ISBNs for historical cartography; GBIF, WoRMS, and iNaturalist taxon keys for species.

---

## 3. Sensitive Species Coordinate Blurring (The 11 km Rule)
To prevent illegal poaching, shell collecting, and unregulated commercial vessel harassment, exact GPS coordinates of endangered and vulnerable species (e.g. *Eretmochelys imbricata*, *Dugong dugon*, *Carcharhinus longimanus*) are **blurred to a ~11 km public bounding grid (~0.1°)**.
* Only verified scientists and accredited park wardens holding active researcher roles may view high-precision coordinates.

---

## 4. Corrections & Amendments Protocol
When factual inaccuracies or revised taxonomies are identified, we do not quietly edit text. A transparent *Correction Notice* is permanently affixed to the article header detailing:
* Date and time of modification.
* Exact nature of the factual change.
* Responsible editor or source confirming the correction.

---

## 5. Regional Basin Neutrality & UN Cartographic Standards
Red Sea Monitor operates as an independent scientific observer monitoring the **entire Red Sea marine basin as a unified, transboundary ecological system**.

* **Sovereign Basin Littoral States:** The platform focuses on marine environments, protected areas, and scientific research across the eight sovereign littoral nations of the Red Sea rift:
  1. Egypt (مصر)
  2. Sudan (السودان)
  3. Saudi Arabia (المملكة العربية السعودية)
  4. Jordan (الأردن)
  5. Eritrea (إريتريا)
  6. Yemen (اليمن)
  7. Djibouti (جيبوتي)
  8. Somalia (الصومال — Gulf of Aden / Bab-el-Mandeb southern fringe baseline)
* **Cartographic Neutrality:** Maritime names and coordinates adhere strictly to UN Cartographic section standards and international maritime hydrography (IHO). Disputed geopolitical territorial claims are handled with complete scientific neutrality, prioritizing benthic ecology, marine currents, and biodiversity.
* **Exclusion of Non-Basin Politicized Claims:** The platform serves sovereign basin scientists and local coastal communities, remaining strictly free from geopolitical polarization.
* **Transboundary Conservation:** Marine species, coral larvae, and ocean currents do not recognize political boundaries. We actively highlight cross-border scientific collaboration and regional agreements (e.g., PERSGA, Regional Action Plans for Coral Reefs and Marine Turtles).

---

## 6. Funding & Independence Disclosure
Red Sea Monitor operates as an open-access editorial and scientific research consortium.
* We do not accept advertising or funding from hotel development conglomerates, extractive fossil fuel projects, or unaccredited commercial tourism operators.
* Institutional partners provide access to research vessels, field stations, and academic peer review without editorial control over our reporting.
